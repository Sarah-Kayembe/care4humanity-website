We love. We care. We teach. We inspire.

Founded by Afreen Dhansay, Care for Humanity was established in 2019 and officially registered as an organization in 2023.

Before its formation, Afreen, along with her family, engaged in humanitarian work. Motivated by their experiences, Afreen decided to provide a platform for the youth to grow and learn to appreciate life's challenges and, importantly, the people around them.

Official Handover of Borehole Donation at Nthwalang Junior Secondary School

Our team proudly visited Nthwalang Junior Secondary School in Digawana Village to officially hand over the borehole we donated. This borehole will serve not only the school but also the entire community, providing a much-needed resource to the village. We were deeply honored to be joined by the village Kgosi (chief), several ministers, MPs, and respected elders, making the handover ceremony a memorable and impactful event for all involved.

Annual Project Warmth: Bringing Comfort to Communities in Need

Our Annual Project Warmth is dedicated to raising donations and purchasing blankets for those in need. This year, we are focused on providing warmth and comfort to communities in need as winter approaches. Each blanket is carefully packed and delivered individually, ensuring that families and individuals facing harsh conditions are supported during the cold months. Your generous contributions make a lasting impact in helping us bring warmth to those who need it most.

Project Necessity: Providing Essential Food Parcels to Families in Need

Project Necessity aims to address food insecurity by distributing essential food parcels to families who need them most. With the rising cost of living and economic challenges, many families struggle to put food on the table. Our initiative ensures that these households receive nutritious food packages that help sustain them during difficult times. Each parcel is carefully curated to provide balanced meals, offering both immediate relief and a sense of hope for a brighter future.

F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How can I contact Care for Humanity?

    You can reach us via email at admin@careforhumanity.co.bw, by phone on +267 71 605 599, on our contact page, or through our social media platforms Care For Humanity. We’re always here to answer any questions or provide more information.

  • How can I donate to Care for Humanity?

    We accept in-kind donations such as food parcels, clothes, hygiene products, or educational materials and more. For monetary donations, please use the following banking details:

    • Bank: First National Bank

    • Branch: Industrial Branch

    • Account Name: Care for Humanity

    • Account Type: Business

    • Account Number: 63075787508

    Please contact us for more details, or visit our Donate page.

  • What kind of activities does Care for Humanity focus on?

    We provide tutoring for children, raise awareness about health issues, organize community clean-ups, and assist with environmental conservation efforts. We also distribute food hampers to families in need and more.

  • How can I support Care for Humanity without donating?

    You can support us by spreading the word about our mission, joining our events, or even volunteering your time to help with our community initiatives.

  • How can I join the Care for Humanity team?

    If you’re passionate about making a difference, we’d love to have you on board! Simply contact us directly, and we’ll guide you through the process.

  • Where are you located?

    We’re based in Gaborone, Botswana, but our work extends throughout the community and sometimes beyond, wherever help is needed.

About Care For Humanity

Care for Humanity, founded by Afreen Dhansay in 2019 and registered in 2023, empowers youth through humanitarian initiatives. Inspired by her family's commitment to service, Afreen established a platform for young individuals to navigate life's challenges while fostering appreciation for their communities. The organization promotes growth, education, and support, making a positive impact on lives.
