Founder & founding director

Afreen Dhansay

Afreen founded Care for Humanity in 2019. Before then, she and her family were already doing humanitarian work, and those experiences inspired her to create a platform for young people to grow, to learn to appreciate life's challenges and, importantly, the people around them.

Under her leadership CFH was officially registered as a non-profit in 2023. In May 2023 she handed over our borehole donation at Nthwalang Junior Secondary School in Digawana, a moment covered by the Daily News.

Volunteer spotlight

Gimhani Mohau Perera

Miss Teen International Botswana 2021

Gimhani rolls up her sleeves with the rest of the team. At Mokolodi she helped pull out invasive plants so grass could grow for the animals.

After being crowned Miss Teen International Botswana 2021, she went on to represent Africa at Miss Teen International 2022, placing in the Top 10 and winning the People's Choice Award.

The team went to the airport to welcome her with the flag of Botswana.

About Care For Humanity

Care for Humanity, founded by Afreen Dhansay in 2019 and registered in 2023, empowers youth through humanitarian initiatives. Inspired by her family's commitment to service, Afreen established a platform for young individuals to navigate life's challenges while fostering appreciation for their communities. The organization promotes growth, education, and support, making a positive impact on lives.
