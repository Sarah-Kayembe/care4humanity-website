About Us

We love. We care. We teach. We inspire.

Humanity starts with Charity

Founded by Afreen Dhansay, Care for Humanity was established in 2019 and officially registered as an organization in 2023.

Before its formation, Afreen, along with her family, engaged in humanitarian work. Motivated by their experiences, Afreen decided to provide a platform for the youth to grow and learn to appreciate life's challenges and, importantly, the people around them.

We are based in Gaborone, Botswana, and work wherever help is needed. Care for Humanity is run by young volunteers who learn by serving others, offering humanitarian aid while building a community with shared interests.

Where we help

About Care For Humanity

Care for Humanity, founded by Afreen Dhansay in 2019 and registered in 2023, empowers youth through humanitarian initiatives. Inspired by her family's commitment to service, Afreen established a platform for young individuals to navigate life's challenges while fostering appreciation for their communities. The organization promotes growth, education, and support, making a positive impact on lives.
