Our programs

What We Do

We provide tutoring for children, raise awareness about health issues, organize community clean-ups, assist with environmental conservation, and distribute food hampers to families in need.

Winter relief

Winter Warmth & Project Warmth

Every year, as the cold months approach, our Annual Project Warmth raises donations and purchases blankets for those in need.

Each blanket is carefully packed and delivered by hand by our volunteers, so that families and individuals facing harsh conditions are supported through winter. Alongside our blanket drives, we also provide essential clothing for people in need.

Hunger relief

Project Necessity & Food Hampers

Project Necessity addresses food insecurity by distributing essential food parcels to the families who need them most. With the rising cost of living, many households struggle to put food on the table.

Each parcel is carefully curated to provide balanced meals, offering both immediate relief and a sense of hope. Our team packed 80 food hampers for the people of Old Naledi, filled with essentials like maize meal, rice and oil, and personally gifted each one.

Education

Empowering Students

Our Tuition Team helps children with their schoolwork and organizes fun, educational events for underprivileged students to inspire and empower them.

Through Swanakids, our Youth Education Mentor teaches and supports young learners. By connecting with students through shared experiences, we foster confidence and a sense of belonging. We are changing the world, one child at a time.

Environment

Wildlife Conservation

We are dedicated to preserving Botswana’s rich wildlife and ensuring a safe environment for animals.

At Mokolodi Nature Reserve, where we volunteered for World Wildlife Day, our Wildlife Team clears invasive plants so grass can grow for the animals, cleans enclosures, and fortifies park boundaries to prevent soil erosion. We also spend time cleaning up national parks.

Water & community

Sustaining Communities

Our team officially handed over the borehole we donated to Nthwalang Junior Secondary School in Digawana Village. It serves the school and the entire village, providing a much-needed resource. We were honored to be joined by the village Kgosi, ministers, MPs and respected elders.

Together with @itsalwaysazeem’s youth organization, we collected female hygiene products and created care packs for the ladies at Mahalapye District Hospital. We also clean the environment and care for animals in shelters.

Health & wellbeing

Health Awareness

We promote health through blood donation drives, awareness about endometriosis and oral hygiene, and fitness initiatives for all. Our group exercise sessions are open to everyone.

Community

Events That Bring Us Together

From CFH bake sales to henna sessions, our events bring people together around a shared purpose.

Each year closes with our Gala Night, where we honor the remarkable efforts of our team members, celebrate achievements and look ahead to the new year.

About Care For Humanity

Care for Humanity, founded by Afreen Dhansay in 2019 and registered in 2023, empowers youth through humanitarian initiatives. Inspired by her family's commitment to service, Afreen established a platform for young individuals to navigate life's challenges while fostering appreciation for their communities. The organization promotes growth, education, and support, making a positive impact on lives.
